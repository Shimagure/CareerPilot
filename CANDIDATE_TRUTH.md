## ANTI-FABRICATION RULES (Enforced Going Forward)

1. **Baseline CV is the single source of truth** for Level 1 claims. Tailored CVs, cover letters, CLAUDE.md "Primary Skills" table, and job postings are NOT evidence.
2. **"Microsoft 365" in baseline ≠ every M365 workload.** Each workload (Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, Intune, Entra ID) must be individually verified.
3. **Metrics (95%, 40%, 50+, 1,000+) require baseline support.** If not in baseline, they are Level 3.
4. **Certifications = Level 2 only.** They do not equate to professional hands-on experience (Level 1).
5. **Cover letter "honest fit" admissions are evidence of gaps.** When a cover letter says "I have not yet used X" or "area I would develop further," that skill is confirmed Level 3.
6. **No keyword stuffing.** ATS optimization never overrides factual accuracy.
